Call the 24/7 Line

Call (817) 623-9267 any hour of any day. A dispatcher answers, asks what you see and gives safety steps while a crew is routed to your address. We do not use online forms, so a call is the quickest way to start.

Have these ready when you call

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street or main road.
  • What happened, where the water came from and whether it has been stopped.
  • Which rooms and materials are wet, such as carpet, hardwood or drywall.
  • Whether anyone has seen mold, sewage or electrical problems.
  • Your insurance carrier name, if you plan to file a claim.

Office and Email

If Water Is Spreading Right Now

Shut off the main water valve, stay out of standing water near outlets and lift valuables off the floor. If you smell gas or see sparking, leave the home and call 911 or the utility before you call us.
