What to Do Before the Crew Arrives, in Any Town

Shut off the main water valve, stay out of standing water near outlets and appliances, and take photos before anything is moved. If you smell gas or see sparking, leave and call 911 or the utility first. The published reference for water damage work is ANSI/IICRC S500, and you can ask any company whether its crews follow it.

Which Service Fits Which Problem

The services are the same in every town. Emergency water removal handles standing water, water mitigation dries wet structures and flood damage restoration covers outside water. For rebuilding, see water damage repair, and for lingering moisture, mold remediation.
