What Causes Water Damage in Lewisville Homes

The causes are the ordinary ones, repeated across a big city. Supply lines fail behind toilets and sinks. Washing machine hoses split. Water heaters leak slowly at the base. Roofs let water into attics, and air conditioner condensate lines back up into ceilings and closets.

Which one you have decides the service. A sudden failure with water on the floor starts with emergency water removal. A leak that was stopped but left wet drywall and insulation is a job for water mitigation, and the rebuild afterward falls under water damage repair.
