Water Damage in Attached Homes at Lakeside DFW

Most Flower Mound housing is single-family. Lakeside DFW is different in one way we can verify. The Town of Flower Mound's homeowner association directory lists Lakeside DFW alongside Lakeside Center Townhomes and Lakeside Tower Condominiums, and its subdivision index includes Lakeside DFW and Lakeside Village sections. Attached homes change how water damage works.

When units share walls, floors or ceilings, a failed supply line, water heater or appliance hose in one home can soak the home next to it or below it. The affected owner often cannot reach the source. That makes the first phone calls as important as the first towel.

How Shared-Wall Drying Is Handled

Crews extract standing water, then use moisture meters to find where it traveled. In attached homes that often means checking the wall shared with the next unit, the ceiling below an upper unit and the baseboards on both sides. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure, and readings are repeated until the materials are dry. Ceilings are opened only where the readings call for it. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the reference to ask a company about.

See water mitigation for the first drying phase and water damage repair for rebuilding drywall and finishes afterward. If the leak came from a tank, water heater leak cleanup covers that case.
